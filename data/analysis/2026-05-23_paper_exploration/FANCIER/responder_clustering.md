# FANCY 1: Responder geographic clustering (Clark-Evans index)

Clark-Evans index = observed mean nearest-neighbor distance / expected under random uniform.
Values: < 0.85 = clustered, ≈ 1.0 = random, > 1.15 = dispersed.
