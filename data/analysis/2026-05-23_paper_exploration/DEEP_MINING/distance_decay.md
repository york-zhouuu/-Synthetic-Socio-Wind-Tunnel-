# Distance-decay of spillover effect (HP variant, 3 seeds pooled)

For each non-protagonist agent, find the **nearest protag-responder**
by home-to-home distance. Bucket by distance, compute responder rate.

If the spillover is truly a 200m spatial mechanism (not selection effect),
we expect a sharp distance-decay: high response near, low response far.

| distance to nearest protag-responder | n non-protag | responders | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-50m | 565 | 148 | 26.2% |
| 50-100m | 257 | 53 | 20.6% |
| 100-150m | 210 | 24 | 11.4% |
| 150-200m | 138 | 6 | 4.3% |
| 200-300m | 136 | 6 | 4.4% |
| 300-400m | 50 | 1 | 2.0% |
| 400-500m | 27 | 0 | 0.0% |
| 500-700m | 9 | 0 | 0.0% |
